Vision & Mission

Guiding principles of Little Flower Primary & High School

Our Vision

To be a leading institution of excellence that nurtures every student's unique potential — fostering intellectual curiosity, moral character, and a lifelong love of learning in a diverse, inclusive community.

Our Mission

To provide a rigorous, holistic education that empowers students with the knowledge, skills, and values required to thrive as responsible citizens and compassionate leaders in a rapidly changing world.
